## ⚠️ The two facts this rests on — verified 31 Jul 2026

1. **Maryland already requires elementary financial-literacy instruction.** In June 2010 the
   State Board adopted regulations requiring local education agencies to provide a program
   of instruction in financial literacy at **elementary, middle and high school**. Not a
   high-school-only mandate. It has been on the books for sixteen years.
2. **HB 943** requires a personal financial literacy course for graduation, effective
   **1 July 2026**, beginning with the **class of 2030**. Anne Arundel County is already
   moving on it for freshmen entering 2026–27.

**So the state just added a high school course on top of an elementary requirement that has
existed since 2010 and has almost nothing built against it.** That is the whole message, it
is Maryland-specific, and he is the person who would know if it is wrong.

**Say "as I read it."** He is the Assistant State Superintendent; inviting the correction is
both honest and the right move. If the regulation has been superseded, he will say so, and
that is still a useful reply.
