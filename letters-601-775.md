the same batch went to `letters-inmail-required.md` as #122–142.

## What was done to the raw drafts

Bodies are the author's own draft text. Four things were added, nothing was rewritten:

- **Sign-off.** `Jonathan` after every letter. 175 of 175.
- **P.S.** The em-dash line, rotated three ways so the same one doesn't land twice in a row.
- **Published status.** 168 of the 175 invite a read or a critique, and every one of those now
  says the book is already printed and on sale — the Karen Holland problem, fixed at the source.
  The other 7 don't ask for anything, so they didn't need the clause.
- **Nothing else.** No grade-band edit was needed: no draft claims K–5 coverage for the book.
  The 131 letters that say "30-year K–5 teacher" are describing Maryann's classroom experience,
  which is K–5. Leave those alone.

Three letters contain the word "empowering" — Omorodion Omokheyeke, Becky House, Kay La' Quel.
In all three it is quoted straight off the recipient's own LinkedIn headline. That's
personalisation, not slop. Don't "fix" them.
