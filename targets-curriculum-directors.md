# Target list — elementary & district curriculum directors

Pulled off three LinkedIn People searches ("director of curriculum elementary" and "director of
curriculum") screenshotted 9 Sep 2026.

**Every result showed as "LinkedIn Member."** That's LinkedIn hiding the names of out-of-network
profiles, not a capture problem. So there are no names here and no letters can be written yet —
but the role, district, location and start date all came through, and that's enough to find the
person by name in about a minute each.

Curriculum directors are arguably a better target than superintendents for this book. A
superintendent forwards it. A curriculum director decides whether it goes in a scope and
sequence.

## How to get the name

Pick whichever is faster for the row:

1. **District website.** Most districts list the curriculum office by name on a staff directory
   page. Fastest for public districts, and it gives you an email as well as a name.
2. **Open the profile from the search.** The View button on each result opens it; the name shows
   on the profile page even when the search result hides it.
3. **Send a connection request**, then capture the name from My Network once accepted. Slowest,
   but it converts the contact to a free DM at the same time.

Once you have a name, add the row to `CGB_MASTER_merged.xlsx` and the letter goes in the running
sequence at 810 onward.
