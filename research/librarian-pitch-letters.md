# Librarian Pitch Letters — Public & School

Drafted July 12, 2026. Companion to `media-pitch-letter.md`.

Two audiences, two letters. The public librarian is buying circulation and
programming. The school librarian is buying curriculum support and a clean
purchase order. Don't send one letter to both; they'll each smell the other
audience in it.

Notes:
- Librarians buy off facts and hate hype. Both letters front-load the
  cataloging data and keep the claims qualified.
- "Age-appropriate" does not appear anywhere below. On purpose.
- Attach nothing. Links carry the freight.

---

## Do-not-alter list (both letters)

- ISBN, LCCN, Lexile, price: exact as written. Never round $19.99.
- Maryann is 30+ years, never 20+. The letters say "30+ years" — keep the plus sign.
- The first-book claim keeps "to our knowledge."
- No Aisle Five reveal, no last-name wordplay, even if a librarian asks for
  the best scene. Tease the orange stickers.
