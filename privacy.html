Privacy Policy

clarencegetsabargain.com · Effective 24 September 2026

The short version

  • The kids’ tools collect nothing. The quiz, the Receipt Builder, the badge maker, and the Wyze Shopper Certificate run entirely in the browser. No accounts, no logins, no analytics, no cookies. A name typed into a certificate never leaves the device.
  • We never sell or rent personal information. We don’t run advertising or retargeting pixels.
  • The only personal information we get is what an adult sends us on purpose: a newsletter signup, a review, or an email.
  • Some adult-facing pages use Google Analytics to count visits. Details below.

Who runs this site

This site is run by Jonathan Bach, author and illustrator of Clarence Gets a Bargain. Questions about this policy go to questions@clarencegetsabargain.com.

Children’s privacy (COPPA)

The book is for kids ages 6–10. This website is for the adults who buy it, read it with them, and teach it: parents, teachers, and librarians. Some pages are interactive tools a child may use with an adult.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The kids’ tools are built so they can’t:

  • Smart Shopper Quiz: answers and the score stay in the browser. “Download My Badge” saves an image to the device. “Share My Score” opens the deviceopens the device’s own share menu; we never see who it goes to.rsquo;s own share menu (or copies the score to the clipboard); we never see who it goes to.
  • Sea-Mart Receipt Builder: all math happens on the device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Wyze Shopper Certificate and the badge maker: the name you type is used to draw the certificate on screen and is not transmitted or stored.

The signup and review forms are for adults. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

For schools and districts

Students never need an account, a login, an email address, or any personal information to use anything on this site. The printable classroom resources are plain web pages you print to PDF. The kids’ tools listed above load no analytics and set no cookies. The only outside request some of them make is for a web font from Google Fonts, which, like any web request, receives the device’s IP address. If your district needs a data privacy agreement or a vendor questionnaire completed, email us.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies of its own. Google Analytics 4 sets first-party cookies (named _ga and _ga_…) to tell one visit from another. It runs only on these pages:

  • the homepage
  • Educator Preview, Zero-Prep Lesson Plans, Curriculum Alignment Matrix, Smart Shopper Challenge, Sea-Mart Secret Mission, and The Money Talk (in /resources/)

It does not run on the kids’ tools. To opt out everywhere, use your browser’s cookie settings or Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. How Google handles this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Other outside services

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and a 3D graphics library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Those services receive your IP address when your browser asks for the file, the same as any website. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs. Links to Facebook, LinkedIn, and Stripe take you to sites with their own policies.

Sharing

We don’t sell personal information. We don’t share it for advertising. We share it only with the services listed above so they can do their job, or if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Newsletter addresses: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Reviews and emails: as long as they’re useful for answering you or featuring the review, and we delete them on request. Google Analytics data: kept for the retention period set in our Google Analytics account, then deleted automatically by Google.

Your choices

Email questions@clarencegetsabargain.com to see, correct, or delete anything you’ve sent us, or to pull a review off the site. Parents can do the same on behalf of a child. We’ll answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new effective date at the top.
