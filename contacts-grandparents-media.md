# Grandparent & Parenting Media — the read-along lane

Built 30 July 2026. Every email below was read off the organization's own published
page on 30 July 2026, not inferred from a pattern. Where there is no published
address, it says so and gives the form instead.

**Why this lane exists.** A grandparent buying a book is buying a way to spend an
afternoon, not an object to hand over. That is the one buyer for whom the read-along
argument *is* the product. And the book is catalogued **Lexile AD 620L** — AD for
**Adult Directed**, the trade's own code for a book meant to be read *with* a child.
The credential does the arguing.

**The date that runs this lane: National Grandparents Day is Sunday, 13 September
2026.** Six weeks out as of this writing. Monthly and bi-monthly outlets are closing
that issue now, so the pitches below go this week or they go next year.

### GRAND Magazine
`grandmagazine.com` · **Christine Crosby, Editorial Director — `ccrosby@grandmagazine.com`**
General submissions: **`editor@grandmagazine.com`**
Contact page states plainly: *"We accept story submissions, product rev[iews]…"*

The only national consumer magazine written for grandparents as grandparents. Its own
topic list names **finance, education, and books.** Sections include Celebrity
GRANDparents, Relationships, Raising Grandchildren, Shop.

**The pitch is a contributed piece, not a book plug.** Title it something like *"The
money lesson your grandkid will actually use on Saturday"* and let the book sit in the
bio line. Send to Crosby with a copy to `editor@`. Grandparents Day is their September
peg; say so in line one.

### More Than Grand — DeeDee Moore
`morethangrand.com` · **`deedee@morethangrand.com`**
~35,000 Instagram followers · ~4,000 email subscribers · placements in HuffPost,
Scary Mommy, MarketWatch, TODAY.com, PureWow, Upworthy, Romper.

She already runs two standing resource pages that are exactly the ask: **"Best Books
on Grandparenting"** and **"Gifts for Grandchildren."** She built the site in 2019
because she could not find grandparent resources she wanted, which is the same
sentence Jonathan says about the money shelf. Lead with that.

She is a grandmother of three and a mother of four, and her whole editorial line is
that modern grandparenting means supporting the parents rather than overriding them.
A book that hands the grandparent a script and lets the parents off the hook fits that
line exactly. **Do not pitch it as a gift.** Pitch it as something to *do* together.

`gu.org` · `grandparentsday.org` · **`gu@gu.org`** · **202-289-3979**

GU runs the official Grandparents Day campaign under the banner **Do Something Grand**

**`lieber@nytimes.com`** — published on his own site at `ronlieber.com/contact`, where

"Your Money" columnist since 2008. Author of ***The Opposite of Spoiled: Raising Kids
