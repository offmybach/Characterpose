# Letters — Gap backlog, Financial Literacy & Services (962–990)

The 29 financial-literacy contacts out of `gap-backlog.md`. All 1st-degree, all free DMs. Titles
from the LinkedIn export of 10 Sep 2026.

**Eleven of the 29 are financial therapists or behavioural money coaches** — Nathan Astle, Chantel
Chapman, Jillian Knight, Kate Grayson, Elana Feinsmith, Heidi Clemons, Stacy Peterson, Simi
Mandelbaum, Stacy Blackshear, DonJay Rice, Derek Hagen. That's a distinct audience and it gets a
distinct argument: they treat adults whose money wounds formed in childhood, so the book sits
upstream of their entire practice. Those letters ask whether a good first money memory protects
anything later — and several invite the obvious objection, that a calm book about a steady
household may only reach children who already have one.

One is British (Brian Byrnes at Moneybox), so his takes the same VAT-versus-sales-tax angle as
the journalist letters.
