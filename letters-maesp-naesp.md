Nine emails. These are **email, not LinkedIn** — every address below is published on the
organisation's own site or in *Principal* magazine's masthead.

Each one argues the spending thesis differently, because a treasurer, an editor, and a
sitting principal do not care about the same sentence. Wally Luckeydoo appears where an
educator credential carries weight and stays out where it would be noise.

Mr. Griffith — a policy observation, offered because it is your beat and it annoys me.

Financial literacy mandates keep passing. Most states have one now. Almost every one of them lands in high school, where the money is, the graduation requirement is, and the political credit is.

2. **Hilliard second.** She is MAESP's NAESP Representative — the single person standing in both doorways. One yes, two organisations.

4. **The MAESP board** — Holbrook, Porter, Messier — same week, different arguments. Holbrook gets the local angle, Porter gets the year she is about to inherit, Messier gets the money first because he is the treasurer.
5. **Knight and Griffith** at NAESP once you have a Maryland yes to point at.
6. **Stephanie Morris last, in late September.** She starts 1 August. Nobody reads anything useful in their first month.
