# Roundup Outreach Kit — "Get Clarence in the Lists"

The single highest-leverage move in the visibility plan (Play 11). Every "best money
books for kids" listicle is what parents read AND what the AI answer engines quote back.
Get on eight of them and you win search + AEO in one stroke.

**The angle that opens the door:** your list is great; here's the one that teaches the
part that comes first. It's a far easier yes than "please add my book," because you're
paying the respect before you ask for anything.

Send these one at a time, personalized. A blast to fifty addresses is a coupon nobody
clips. Ten real emails beat a hundred templated ones.

---

## Before you hit send — the 30-second checklist

1. Read their actual list. Name it in line one. Name one book on it you genuinely like.
2. Swap every `[bracket]`. If a bracket ships, you've lost.
3. Offer the hardback, not a PDF. A book on a desk gets reviewed; a PDF gets a "thanks."
4. One ask per email. The ask is: add it, or take a review copy. Nothing else.
5. Mean the compliment. If you don't rate their list, don't write them.

**Cadence:** five a week, personalized, tracked in a simple sheet (sent / opened / replied /
copy mailed). Follow up once at day 7. Then let it rest. A review copy in the mail does more
persuading than a third email ever will.

**One rule:** never fake the compliment and never send the same email twice. The whole
angle only works if you actually respect the list you're asking to join.
