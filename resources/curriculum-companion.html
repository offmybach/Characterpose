Curriculum Companion — Classroom Printables

Five ready-to-use printables for the Clarence Gets a Bargain 4-week financial literacy module. Print all or jump to one:

1. Sort Cards 2. Comparison Worksheet 3. Clearance Stickers 4. Price Tags 5. 10% OFF Coupons

Tip: in the print dialog, use "Pages" to print just the one you need. Color printing recommended — the chips, stickers, and coupons rely on it. (Black-and-white will still work, but the kids will miss the orange.)
